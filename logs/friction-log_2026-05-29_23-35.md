## Friction Points

### F1: Initial Docker push throughput appeared stuck

- **Step:** 5 (Build and Push Container Images)
- **Severity:** Low
- **What happened:** The `openrouteservice` image's 159 MB layer and the concurrent `ors_control_app` push appeared frozen on a single carriage-return progress frame (`Pushing 16.78MB → 17.83MB` over several minutes), and `list-images` returned "No data" for ~15 min. This matched the symptom signature of the documented SPCS registry token-refresh hang (AGENTS.md crane workaround).
- **Resolution:** Followed the build-images.md guidance to "verify completion separately, do not assume stuck." Measured byte delta over a precise 90 s window: ~46 MB combined → ~26 MB/min, confirming it was bandwidth/ramp-up, NOT the token-refresh hang. Let both pushes finish naturally; all 5 images registered. `crane` was available as a fallback but not needed.
- **Recommendation:** Add a note to `build-images.md` Step 4 quantifying expected throughput (e.g., "first push of the 159 MB ORS layer takes 8-12 min; measure a 90 s byte delta before suspecting the token-refresh hang"). Optionally advise building/pushing the large `openrouteservice` image serially (not concurrently with `ors_control_app`) so neither starves the uplink and progress is easier to read.
