- **What happened:** Staged config with `init_threads: 3` caused ORS container `lastExitCode: 1` and restart loops on `ORS_SERVICE_SANFRANCISCO` (2G XMS).
- **Resolution:** Cap by compute tier in `WRITE_ORS_CONFIG`: S→2, L→4, XXL→8; staged SF default set to `init_threads: 2`.
